The shop files are stored as shops-common.txt and shops-renewal.txt.
Derived prices.json and shops.json are rebuilt by the import scripts.
Sell is used if present; otherwise floor(Buy / 2), per upstream schema.
Buy is retained only when explicitly present. Shop prices use an explicit shop
override or that exact item Buy value; missing prices stay unknown.
Entries with neither price are omitted: no confirmed Zero Global price is inferred.
Only matching item ID and Aegis identity may use a reference price.
Shop NPCs, maps and coordinates are rAthena common and Renewal references and are not
automatically verified for Zero Global. No player-market prices. Full license: LICENSE.
